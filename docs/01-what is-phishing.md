# What is Phishing?
Phishing is a social engineering attack where criminals pretend to be a trusted person or organisation to trick you into revealing sensitive information, clicking a malicious link or opening an infected attachment.
## Why it works
Attackers use urgency, fear, authority and curiosity to make people act before thinking
## What attackers want
- Passwords and login details
- Financial or personal information
- Access to systems through malware
